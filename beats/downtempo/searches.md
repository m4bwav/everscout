## What not to trust

Chill-playlist SEO farms and playlist-pitching services; mood-music channels on YouTube, many AI-generated; "top 50 downtempo artists" lists; AI-written genre explainers (one invented a 2026 Bonobo album); gear-shop "producer secrets" advertorials.
