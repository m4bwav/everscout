# Question bank: down-tempo, trip-hop and chill electronic music

Seeds for everscout-engage, asked of producers who share their own tracks. Name the moment you mean ("the pad that comes in at 1:20"). Never ask whether a track used AI.

## How to use this file

One observation about the sound, then one or two questions. Producers answer gear, sampling and texture questions at length; keep release-strategy questions for people who mention a release.

## Gear and DAW

- [gear] What was the main instrument or sampler on this, in the box or hardware?
- [gear] Which DAW, and do you start in a loop or arrange straight away?

## Sampling

- [sampling] Is the main loop a sample or played?
- [sampling] How did you chop it: slices, pitch, time-stretch?
- [sampling] Where do you dig for source material?

## Sound design

- [sound] How did you get that tape or vinyl warmth, a plugin chain or real tape?
- [sound] What's on the drum bus?
- [sound] Which reverb is giving it that space?

## Mixing

- [mixing] How do you keep the low end clean at slow tempos?
- [mixing] What loudness do you master to for streaming versus Bandcamp?
- [mixing] How did you sit the vocal back in the mix like that?

## Composition

- [composition] What tempo and key, and how did the track start?
- [composition] How do you keep a slow track moving over five minutes?
- [composition] Which records were you using as references?

## Release

- [release] Self-released or on a label? How did that come about?
- [release] Where do listeners actually find you, Bandcamp or streaming?
- [release] Who made the artwork?
- [release] Do you play it live, and with what setup?

## Background

- [background] Who got you into trip-hop or down-tempo?
- [background] What are you listening to right now?
