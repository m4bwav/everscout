# Literature: AI voice

The books of this beat, in three tiers (`kb/literature.md`). AI voice has almost no canon of its own, so the canon is the older craft and science it rests on. Listing only; nothing bought or downloaded. Phonetics leads not listed: Ladefoged, Stevens, Titze, Wells. Actor voice leads not listed: Berry, Rodenburg, Blumenfeld.
