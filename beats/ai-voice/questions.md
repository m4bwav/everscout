# Question bank: AI voice

Seeds for everscout-engage (format: `beats/_template/questions.md`). Tag `ai` marks seeds that name models or generation; drop them where a community is hostile. Rephrase each for the post and tie it to a moment the maker showed.

## How to use this file

Two or three seeds from different categories. Name the line or moment you mean. Only in engine communities; never in r/VoiceActing or r/audiobooks.

## Engines

- [engines, ai] Which engine did this come from, and which version?
- [engines, ai] What did you try first and drop, and why?
- [engines, ai] How much VRAM and how long per minute of speech?

## Range

- [range, ai] How did you get away from the stock voices: reference clip, blending, conversion, or prompt alone?
- [range, ai] How many different voices could you get before they started to sound alike?

## Consistency

- [consistency, ai] Does the voice hold across sessions, and what do you pin to rebuild it?
- [consistency, ai] How long a chunk do you generate before the voice wanders?

## Quality

- [quality, ai] Did any denoiser or enhancer make it worse?
- [quality] What did you do about room tone and level between lines?
- [quality] Did you measure the take, and with the same settings each time?
- [quality, ai] Is the breath or noise in the voice itself, or added over it afterwards?

## Craft and reception

- [craft] How did you direct the emotion on this line?
- [reception] Did you disclose it, and how did people react?
