# Question bank: global economics and macro

Seeds for everscout-engage, asked of people who post a chart, a paper, a dataset or an analysis. Rephrase for the post and name the chart or table you mean. Never argue the conclusion; ask about the evidence.

## How to use this file

Two or three seeds from different categories. Economists answer data and method questions readily and dislike being told they are wrong in a comment; ask what would change their mind instead.

## Data

- [data] Which series is that, and do you have the source or FRED id?
- [data] Is it seasonally adjusted, and by whom?
- [data] Which vintage is this, before or after the latest revision?
- [data] How did you deflate it: CPI, PCE or the GDP deflator?
- [data] Why that base year or starting point?

## Method

- [method] Is that year on year, annualised month on month, or a three-month average?
- [method] Is the trend line a filter or a moving average?
- [method] How did you handle the pandemic-era outliers?
- [method] Does the relationship hold with a different sample window?
- [method] What is the identification here: a difference in differences, an event study?

## Interpretation

- [interpretation] What would change your read of this?
- [interpretation] How much of the move is composition?
- [interpretation] Is the tariff pass-through measured at the border or at retail?
- [interpretation] How does it compare across countries, given the definitions differ?

## Tools and craft

- [tools] What do you make the charts with?
- [tools] Would you share the code or the spreadsheet?
- [tools] How do you keep the data pulls up to date around the release calendar?

## Research

- [research] Is the paper a working paper or through review, and is there a replication package?
- [research] What surprised you most in the data?
- [research] Who else is working on this that I should read?
