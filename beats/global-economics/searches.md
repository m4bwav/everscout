# Web searches: global economics and macro

Run by everscout-scan for the period since the last scan (add the month and year). Central bank meeting dates matter more than the calendar week: scan the week after a Fed, ECB or BoE decision.

| query | cadence | yields |
|---|---|---|
| FOMC statement <month year>, ECB monetary policy decision <month year>, Bank of England MPC decision <month year> | per meeting | rate moves, votes and dissents |
| CPI release <month year> core inflation | monthly | inflation prints and readings |
| jobs report nonfarm payrolls <month year> | monthly | labour market |
| tariff ruling OR "Section 122" OR "Section 232" <month year> | weekly | trade policy changes |
| Brent oil price energy shock inflation <month year> | weekly | the energy shock |
| NBER working paper <topic> <month year> | weekly | new research |
| "chart of the week" economy <month year> | weekly | chart makers to follow |
| IMF World Economic Outlook <month year> | April, July, October, January | global forecasts |
| OECD Economic Outlook interim <month year> | quarterly | forecasts |
| sovereign debt restructuring <year> | monthly | development and emerging markets |

## What not to trust

Partisan outlets and think tanks without saying their lean; "collapse" blogs; viral posts from r/economy and finance-meme accounts; charts without source or units, or with truncated axes; nominal numbers read as real; a single month read as a trend; preliminary data without revisions; "consensus" figures without a source; AI-written summary sites that misstate figures; paraphrases of paywalled headlines.
