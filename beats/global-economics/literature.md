## Canon

The most important books of all time for this subject (yearly recheck).

| title | authors | year | id | why | evidence | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Wealth of Nations | Adam Smith | 1776 | OL76827W | Set as reading in university economic-thought courses such as UConn's; it founded the study of markets, specialisation and trade. | https://richard-langlois.uconn.edu/?p=226 | lead |
| The General Theory of Employment, Interest and Money | John Maynard Keynes | 1936 | OL35951W | Required reading on historic Columbia and Chicago monetary economics lists; it created macroeconomics as a field. | https://www.irwincollier.com/2015/10/ | lead |
| A Monetary History of the United States, 1867-1960 | Milton Friedman, Anna Schwartz | 1963 | OL2747776W | The core text of Rutgers' course on US monetary history; it made central bank mistakes central to explaining recessions. | https://econweb.rutgers.edu/bordo/econ344.html | lead |
| Capital in the Twenty-First Century | Thomas Piketty | 2013 | OL16814568W | It put long-run wealth inequality data at the centre of economic debate. | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times_Business_Book_of_the_Year_Award | lead |
| This Time Is Different | Carmen Reinhart, Kenneth Rogoff | 2009 | OL18666643W | Required reading on financial-crisis courses at Kiel and Notre Dame; it gave the standard dataset and history of debt and banking crises. | https://www.ifw-kiel.de/fileadmin/Dateiverwaltung/IfW_Unit/Education/Outlines/2018-19/Reinhart_Kiel_2018_syl.pdf | lead |
| Globalization and Its Discontents | Joseph Stiglitz | 2002 | OL624826W | Required reading on a university course on the political economics of globalisation; the best-known insider critique of IMF crisis policy. | https://efst.unist.hr/dokumenti/sylabus/EUE302en.pdf | lead |
| Why Nations Fail | Daron Acemoglu, James Robinson | 2012 | OL16568759W | A required text on development-economics syllabi such as Carleton's; it argues institutions explain why some countries grow and others do not. | https://carleton.ca/economics/wp-content/uploads/sites/112/2026/08/ECON3508_Vivek_F26.pdf | lead |
| Lords of Finance | Liaquat Ahamed | 2009 | OL13650534W | Won the 2010 Pulitzer Prize for History and the 2009 FT business book award; it shows how four central bankers turned a crash into the Depression. | https://www.pulitzer.org/winners/liaquat-ahamed | lead |
| Crashed | Adam Tooze | 2018 | OL19752591W | Won the 2019 Lionel Gelber Prize and made NYT and FT books-of-the-year lists; the standard global history of the 2008 crisis. | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Tooze | lead |
