# Question bank: {{TITLE}}

Seeds for the questions the everscout-engage skill improvises. Each line is `- [tags] question`; `everscout.py questions --beat {{SLUG}} --n 4 --tags a,b` samples across categories and skips seeds used in the last 30 days. Never paste a seed as written: rephrase it for the post, in the user's voice, tied to something the maker actually showed. Seeds tagged `ai` are dropped by `--avoid-ai`.

## How to use this file

Two to four seeds from at least two categories. Lead with a short true compliment, then the questions. Ask how something was made, never whether it was generated. Fifteen to forty seeds in three to seven categories.
