# Question bank: Personal and professional websites

Seeds for the questions everscout-engage improvises. Format and rules: `beats/_template/questions.md`. The maker is someone who showed their own site. Ask about decisions and results, never whether it was made with AI. Built 2026-09-27.

## How to use this file

Two to four seeds from at least two categories. Lead with a short, specific thanks for something on the site.

## Purpose

- [purpose] What did you want this site to do for you when you started the redesign?
- [purpose] Has the site ever led to a job, a client or a conversation you would not have had otherwise?
- [purpose] Who do you picture reading it: recruiters, peers, clients, or yourself?
- [purpose] Why keep a site of your own rather than only a LinkedIn profile?

## Content

- [content] What did you cut from the previous version, and was that hard?
- [content] How did you decide which projects to show and in what order?
- [content] Do you write case studies, and how long do they end up?
- [content] How often do you update it, and what keeps you updating?
- [content] Do you have a now, uses or colophon page, and do people read it?
- [content] Does anyone edit your writing before it goes up, and how do you settle a disagreement?
- [content] Which odd style choices do you keep on purpose, and has a reader ever tripped on one?

## Design

- [design] Where did the main visual idea come from?
- [design] How did you choose the type, and what did you try first?
- [design] How did you balance personality against getting to the work quickly?
- [design] Did you test it with anyone before launching, and what changed?
- [design] How did you handle dark mode and accessibility?
- [design] What did a first-time visitor misread or miss, and what did you change?
- [design] Which links open a new tab, download a file or open mail, and how does the site say so first?

## Build

- [build] What is it built with, and would you choose the same again?
- [build, ai] Did you use any AI tools while building it, and where did they help or get in the way?
- [build] Where is it hosted, and what does it cost you a year?
- [build] How long did the redesign take, start to launch?

## Small web

- [smallweb] Do you offer RSS, and do you see people subscribing?
- [smallweb] Are you in any webrings or blogrolls, and did they bring visitors?
- [smallweb] Do you post on your site first and syndicate elsewhere?

## AI and design

- [ai, design] Where in the design did AI help most, and where did you override what it suggested?
- [ai, design] How did you keep the site from looking like every other AI-built one?
- [ai, design] Did you write down a design direction (tokens, fonts, rules) for the tool to follow, and did it stick to it?
- [ai] Has anyone used the chat or AI feature on your site, and what do they ask it?
- [ai] Does the AI feature say it is an AI, and what does it do when it cannot answer?

## Type

- [type] Which typeface did you settle first, and how did the others follow from it?
- [type] When you changed your line length or text size, what else did you have to change to keep it readable?
- [type] Did you test the type by reading a long post on a phone, and what did you change after?
- [type] If your sizes are fluid, did you test them at 200% zoom, and what did you change?
- [type] If you moved to a variable font, did the total download shrink, and which axes do you use?
- [type] When you compared typefaces, did you set the same text at the same size, measure and line-height?
- [type] When you stepped back from a page of text, what uneven spacing did you find?
- [type] Which empty areas of your page did you design on purpose?
- [type] Did you check your font's kerning and figures in your own language and with your own numbers?

## Forms

- [forms] Which questions did you cut from your contact form, and did more or better messages come in?
- [forms] What does someone see after your form sends, and do they know when you will reply?
