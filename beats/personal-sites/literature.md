## Canon

The most important books of all time for this subject (yearly recheck).

| title | authors | year | id | why | evidence | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Don't Make Me Think | Steve Krug | 2000 | OL8578577W | It is the usability book most often named as the first read for site builders. | https://www.justinmind.com/web-design/books | lead |
| The Elements of Typographic Style | Robert Bringhurst | 1992 | OL854086W | It is the standard typography reference behind the text-first personal site. | https://www.elegantthemes.com/blog/resources/essential-typography-books | lead |
| Thinking with Type | Ellen Lupton | 2004 | OL2969855W | It is the most approachable typography text and is ranked first in community threads. | https://blog.prototypr.io/7-best-design-books-on-typography-b4a44447731d | lead |
| Responsive Web Design | Ethan Marcotte | 2011 | OL15692545W | Judgment call: it defined the layout approach every site now assumes. | https://openlibrary.org/works/OL15692545W | lead |
| Designing with Web Standards | Jeffrey Zeldman | 2003 | OL6045343W | Judgment call: it made the case for semantic, standards-based pages that still underpins the hand-built site. | https://openlibrary.org/works/OL6045343W | lead |
| Show Your Work! | Austin Kleon | 2014 | OL16821630W | Judgment call: it gives the reasoning for sharing work in public, which is why many people keep a personal site at all. | https://openlibrary.org/works/OL16821630W | lead |
| Web Typography | Richard Rutter | 2017 | OL27054557W | It condenses typography practice for the browser. | https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/35429034-web-typography | lead |
