## What not to trust

- Trend lists from site builders (Wix, Framer, Squarespace) sell their templates; count a trend only when independent sources agree.
- "Top portfolio" galleries favour spectacle; they show what gets featured, not what gets someone hired.
- "Roast my portfolio" posts with templated replies.
