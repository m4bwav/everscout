## What not to trust

Vendor launch reels (cherry-picked, retries hidden); "best AI video generator 2026" lists written by resellers, which disagree on versions; benchmark claims without confidence intervals; "which is best" threads seeded by aggregators; any "open source" claim before the licence is read.
