## Perspectives to research from

- A newcomer with a laptop and a budget: where to start and what it costs.
- A working maker who ships monthly: what changed this year and what still breaks.
- A film editor or cinematographer sceptical of AI: what is lost and what the work still needs.
- A platform or festival: rules, disclosure, audience response.
