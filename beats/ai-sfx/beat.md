# AI and scripted sound effects: models, tools and craft

Follows people who make sound effects for games, film and other media, by model, by synthesis, by microphone or from a library, and those who judge the results. It feeds sfxwright. What people ship and what sound designers and players accept matters more than launch news.

Split with the other beats: `ai-voice` keeps speech and singing. `ai-video` keeps audio native to a video model (Veo, Kling, MiniMax H3, LTX) as part of a video pipeline; a model made to add sound to a finished silent video (HunyuanVideo-Foley, ControlFoley) is here. Music models stay out unless a post tests them on effects, stingers or instrument one-shots.

## Scope

- In: text-to-sound and audio-to-audio models with reported results and samples; ComfyUI workflows; video-to-sound and automatic foley; procedural and synthesized sound; game audio tools and middleware; sound libraries and their licences; naming and metadata; layering, editing and delivery craft; reaction to AI sound; store rules, copyright and sound marks.
- Out: soundboards and meme clips; vendor listicles; music production with no effects angle; speech; generic AI news; job posts.

## What we want to learn

1. Which routes do people finish real sounds with, for each job (one-shots, impacts, UI, footsteps, ambiences, creatures, machines, magic, retro, stingers, sound from video)?
2. What makes generated sound thin, muddy or samey, and which fixes work (prompt words, layering, pitch, stretching, convolution, seed fishing)?
3. How do people build variation sets that do not machine-gun and still read as one sound?
4. Which edits can be scripted well, and which still need a person?
5. What reads as AI or as an overused library sound to designers and audiences?
6. How are licences, stores and courts treating generated audio and library sounds, and which rules changed?

## Keep and skip

- Keep: a comparison with samples; a model or node release thread with reported results; a sound breakdown naming layers and processing; a game or film post on how its sound was made; a designer's specific objection; a licence, watermark or store change; a ruling.
- Skip: "what is the best AI SFX tool" with no answers worth reading, vendor reels, resold wrappers, promotion of a sound pack with no method.

## Engagement notes

The sound design and game audio communities are wary of AI and many are small, so a stranger's process questions would cost more than they bring. r/comfyui, r/StableDiffusion and r/LocalLLaMA expect tool questions if the user later wants to ask there.
