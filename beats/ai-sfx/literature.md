# Literature: AI and scripted sound effects

The books of this beat, in three tiers (`kb/literature.md`). AI sound effects have no canon of their own, so the canon is the older craft and science they rest on. Evidence is one publisher or bookseller page per row, so these stay leads until a syllabus or practitioner list backs them. Listing only; nothing bought or downloaded.
