# Question bank: AI and scripted sound effects

Seeds for everscout-engage (format: `beats/_template/questions.md`). The beat starts read-only. Tag `ai` marks seeds that name models or generation; use them only in model communities. Rephrase each for the post and tie it to a moment the maker showed.

## How to use this file

Two or three seeds from different categories. Name the sound or moment you mean. Never put an `ai` seed to r/sounddesign, r/GameAudio, r/AudioPost or r/foley.

## Process

- [process] What were the separate layers in this sound, and which one came first?
- [process] What did you start from for the tail: a recording, a synth, or a stretched copy of the attack?
- [process] How many takes did you throw away before this one?
- [process] Did you distort the layers one by one or the whole mix, and could you hear the difference?
- [process] Did you judge this sound alone or against the picture, and did the picture change your choice?
- [process] How much friction or grit did you leave in, and did taking it out make the sound feel fake or cleaner?
- [process] What did you cut last, and why did it go?
- [process] How do you decide when a sound is finished?
- [process] What sounded right in the editor and wrong once it was in the game?
- [process] Where is your listener, on the camera or the character, and what did that change in the sounds you made?
- [process] Which part of this sound seemed thin alone but worked in the scene, and how did you judge it?
- [process] When a generated sound came out thin, what fixed it apart from layering: EQ, distortion, envelope or something else?
- [process] Which noise colour or inharmonic source did you start this from, and why that one?
- [process] Which physical process makes this sound, and did you build from that or from a reference recording?
- [process] What does each layer's decay do here, and which layer outlasts the others?

- [process] Which sound did you cut because it sat alone with nothing around it?
- [process] When did a familiar wrong sound beat an accurate one, and did generated audio change that?
- [process] Which of your sounds must a player hear to play well, and what replaces them when the game is muted?

## Variation and sets

- [variation] How many variations did this event need before players stopped noticing the repeat?
- [variation] What do you randomize in the engine, and what do you bake into the files?
- [variation] How far can you push pitch here before it stops being the same object?
- [variation] How do you keep a whole game's footsteps in the same world?
- [variation] How many files does a sound played thousands of times get, compared with one played fifty times?
- [variation] Do you detune the whole sound between takes, or only one layer?
- [variation] Does a harder hit change only the level here, or its attack and tail too?

- [tools] How do you name and tag files so you can find them in a year?
- [tools] What do you script, and what do you always do by hand?

## Rights and reception

- [reception] What feedback from players or the director changed the sound?
