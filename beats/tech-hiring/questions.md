# Question bank: Developer and tech hiring

Seeds for the questions the everscout-engage skill improvises. Format and rules: `beats/_template/questions.md`. On this beat the "maker" is someone who published their own search data or their own hiring process. Ask about the numbers and the method; never ask for their employer, their identity or anything that would out them, and never ask whether a post was written with AI. Built 2026-09-26 from the shape of questions that drew long answers in search-report and hiring-manager threads.

## How to use this file

Two to four seeds from at least two categories. Lead with a short, true thanks for sharing the numbers, then the questions.

## Search process (candidates)

- [search, data] Roughly how many applications did it take to get the first interview, and did that ratio change as you went?
- [search, channels] Which channel produced the offers in the end: referrals, recruiters reaching out, job boards, or your own network?
- [search] How long was it from the first application to the signed offer?
- [search, data] Did you tailor each application, or send a standard one, and could you see a difference in the response rate?
- [search, channels] Did applying within the first day or two of a posting make a difference for you?
- [search] What would you do differently if you started the search again tomorrow?
- [search, ai] Did you use any tools to find or apply to roles, and did they help or hurt?

## Interviews

- [interviews] Which round was the hardest, and was it the one you expected?
- [interviews] How did the take-home or live-coding round compare with a LeetCode-style round, from your side?
- [interviews, ai] Were you allowed to use an AI assistant in any round, and how did they grade that round?
- [interviews] How did you prepare, and how much time did preparation take a week?
- [interviews] Did any company tell you why you were turned down, and was it useful?
- [interviews, ai] Did any interview happen in person because of cheating worries, and how did that change the day?

## Hiring side (managers and recruiters)

- [hiring] About how many applications does one opening get now, and how many of them does a person actually read?
- [hiring, ai] What part of screening is automated where you are, and what do you still do by hand?
- [hiring] What makes a résumé get a call in the first ten seconds?
- [hiring, ai] Have you changed your interview format because of AI assistants, and what did you change it to?
- [hiring] What signal do you look for in a junior candidate now that you did not look for three years ago?
- [hiring] How do you tell a real candidate from a fake or proxy one?
- [hiring] What would you tell candidates about referrals: do they still move the needle?

## Market and data

- [data] Where did the numbers come from, and how did you count them?
- [data] What would change your mind about the trend you describe?
- [data] Does what you see match the postings data (Indeed, TrueUp, JOLTS), or does it disagree?
- [market] Which specialties or levels are you seeing demand for right now, and which have gone quiet?
- [market] Has remote, hybrid or return-to-office changed which offers people take?

## Outcomes and reception

- [outcome] What did the offer look like against what you expected, and did you negotiate?
- [outcome] Looking back, what mattered most in getting the job?
