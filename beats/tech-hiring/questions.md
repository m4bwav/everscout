# Question bank: Developer and tech hiring

Seeds for the questions the everscout-engage skill improvises. Format and rules: `beats/_template/questions.md`. On this beat the "maker" is someone who published their own search data or their own hiring process. Ask about the numbers and the method; never ask for their employer, their identity or anything that would out them, and never ask whether a post was written with AI. Built 2026-09-26 from the shape of questions that drew long answers in search-report and hiring-manager threads.

## How to use this file

Two to four seeds from at least two categories. Lead with a short, true thanks for sharing the numbers, then the questions.

## Search process (candidates)

- [search, data] Roughly how many applications did it take to get the first interview, and did that ratio change as you went?
- [search, channels] Which channel produced the offers in the end: referrals, recruiters reaching out, job boards, or your own network?
- [search] How long was it from the first application to the signed offer?
- [search, data] Did you tailor each application, or send a standard one, and could you see a difference in the response rate?
- [search, channels] Did applying within the first day or two of a posting make a difference for you?
- [search] What would you do differently if you started the search again tomorrow?
- [search, ai] Did you use any tools to find or apply to roles, and did they help or hurt?

## Interviews

- [interviews] Did an interviewer push back on an answer you later found was correct, and how did you read it at the time?
- [interviews] Which round was the hardest, and was it the one you expected?
- [interviews] How did the take-home or live-coding round compare with a LeetCode-style round, from your side?
- [interviews, ai] Were you allowed to use an AI assistant in any round, and how did they grade that round?
- [interviews] How did you prepare, and how much time did preparation take a week?
- [interviews] Did any company tell you why you were turned down, and was it useful?
- [interviews, ai] Did any interview happen in person because of cheating worries, and how did that change the day?
- [interviews] Did you ever play down something you had done because claiming it felt like bragging, and did it seem to cost you?
- [interviews] When you pushed back on an interviewer in a design round, did it read as engagement or as attitude?
- [interviews] Were you coached to sound more direct or stop hedging, and did it change how the interviewers responded?
- [interviews] Did giving candidates the questions or the exercise ahead of time change who did well?
- [interviews] Were you ever told you were not a strong enough leader or not confident enough, with no evidence from your work?

## Hiring side (managers and recruiters)

- [hiring] When you challenge a candidate's answer, what are you testing, and does it change with other interviewers in the room?
- [hiring] About how many applications does one opening get now, and how many of them does a person actually read?
- [hiring, ai] What part of screening is automated where you are, and what do you still do by hand?
- [hiring] What makes a résumé get a call in the first ten seconds?
- [hiring, ai] Have you changed your interview format because of AI assistants, and what did you change it to?
- [hiring] What signal do you look for in a junior candidate now that you did not look for three years ago?
- [hiring] How do you tell a real candidate from a fake or proxy one?
- [hiring] What would you tell candidates about referrals: do they still move the needle?
- [hiring] When a candidate says "we built X", how does your loop find out what they did themselves, and does it work as well for modest talkers?
- [hiring] In promotion or level calibration, what evidence counts for incidents someone prevented, against incidents they fixed?
- [hiring, ai] If your screen or AI ranker uses signals from outside the CV (GitHub activity, open-source work, side projects), have you measured who passes by group and by whether they have care duties?
- [hiring] Who records why each raise or promotion was given, and does anyone review those decisions as a set?
- [hiring] Do people who want promotion get the written criteria and the names of the decision makers, or do they learn them by asking?
- [hiring] Of the people promoted or hired into a first manager role last year, how many were women, and does anyone compare that with the pool below?
- [hiring] When was pay last audited by gender and level, and was a gap found by audit or by a leak?
- [interviews] How long do your interviewers wait after a question before rephrasing or moving on, and does anyone track which candidates got cut off?
- [interviews] When a candidate's answer seemed to have no point, did a second interviewer or the written notes agree, or was the point there in a different order?
- [hiring] When you suspect a rating reflects a candidate's accent, style or background more than the answer, what do you do: re-score, hold the verdict, or ask for a work sample or a second interviewer?
- [hiring] Which résumé signals make a candidate feel credible at once (an employer, a school, a GitHub profile), and how often has that first impression turned out wrong?
- [hiring, ai] For an AI screener or interviewer, whose way of speaking was it taught to treat as clear, and who checks whether a weak answer is a gap in the rubric rather than in the candidate?

- [hiring] Do your panel debriefs check who actually knew the material against who seemed to lead?
- [hiring] Does the round allow only turn-taking or open discussion too, and have you seen who speaks more in each?
- [hiring] When a scorecard says a candidate lacked confidence or was too aggressive, what did they actually say, and would the same words get the same note from someone else?
- [hiring] Do you still use a personality test to shortlist, and has anyone checked it against how those hires did?
- [hiring] When you promote someone to lead, do you ask the people who would report to them, and has that ever changed the decision?

## Market and data

- [data] Where did the numbers come from, and how did you count them?
- [data] What would change your mind about the trend you describe?
- [data] Are the numbers split by sex (or another group) at each stage: applied, screened, interviewed, offered, accepted?
- [data] Does what you see match the postings data (Indeed, TrueUp, JOLTS), or does it disagree?
- [market] Which specialties or levels are you seeing demand for right now, and which have gone quiet?
- [market] Has remote, hybrid or return-to-office changed which offers people take?
- [market] Was the remote or flexible option in the job ad, or did you have to ask for it, and did asking cost you anything?

## AI at work (managers and juniors)

- [ai-work, juniors] Which work that used to go to juniors do seniors now hand to AI, and how do you plan to grow your next mid-level engineers?
- [ai-work, screening] If AI narrows the gap between strong and weak performers, what do you screen for now, and has it changed what experience or a degree is worth in your offers?
- [ai-work, screening] When AI ranks the résumés or grades the tests, how do reviewers keep from just accepting its picks?
- [ai-work, interviews] With take-homes impossible to police, what replaced them: a live round, a supervised one, or AI allowed and you defend the answer?

## Outcomes and reception

- [outcome] What did the offer look like against what you expected, and did you negotiate?
- [outcome] Looking back, what mattered most in getting the job?
- [outcome] Did you ever ask directly about promotion, and what made that easy or hard?
- [outcome] Was there an interview that felt unfair before you had a word for why, and what term later named it?
