# Web searches: Developer and tech hiring

What the scan runs with the agent's web search beyond the feeds, scoped to the period since the last scan (add the month and year). Built 2026-09-26.

| query | cadence | yields |
|---|---|---|
| software engineer job postings <month year> | weekly | postings data and the coverage of it |
| tech layoffs <month year> | weekly | new layoffs and who cited AI |
| technical interview AI cheating in-person <month year> | weekly | interview-format changes at named companies |
| "AI interviewer" OR "AI interview" hiring candidates <month year> | monthly | AI-run screening and candidate reactions |
| new grad software engineer hiring <year> | monthly | entry-level demand and new-grad programmes |
| H-1B OR OPT rule change tech <month year> | monthly | visa policy that moves tech hiring |
| AI hiring law automated employment decision <month year> | monthly | regulation of AI screening |
| return to office mandate tech <month year> | monthly | RTO as a hiring and attrition factor |
| ghost jobs study OR data <year> | quarterly | how many postings are real |
| software engineer compensation report <year> | quarterly | pay data (Levels.fyi, Dice, Hired-style reports) |
| boomerang rehire laid off engineers <year> | quarterly | rehiring after cuts |

## What not to trust

SEO listicles ("best job boards 2026"), "I analyzed 10,000 jobs" posts that end in a product link, job-tool vendors' "reports" built on their own users, bootcamp outcomes pages, résumé-writing services, single anecdotes presented as the market, any statistic without a date and a source. Prefer the postings indexes, official statistics, named-company announcements, and candidates or managers posting their own numbers.
