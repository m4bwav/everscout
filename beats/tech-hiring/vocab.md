# Vocabulary: Developer and tech hiring

The entities the tally counts. Format and rules: `beats/_template/vocab.md` and `kb/SCHEMA.md`. Built 2026-09-26 from post titles in r/cscareerquestions, r/csMajors, r/leetcode, r/recruitinghell, r/ExperiencedDevs and r/cscareerquestionsEU on that date, and from Indeed Hiring Lab and Pragmatic Engineer headlines. Company names are left out on purpose: they would swamp the tally; a note names the company in its text.

## How to use this file

Keep canonical ids stable: renaming one splits its history in the tallies. A new interview format or tool gets its own id when people compare it with the old one.
