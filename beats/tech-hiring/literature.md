## Canon

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who: The A Method for Hiring | Geoff Smart, Randy Street | 2008 | OL8748041W | It is the most cited structured interviewing method among hiring managers. | https://medium.com/hiresweet/first-time-hiring-tech-talent-here-are-the-books-you-must-absolutely-read-be4aa4e1c19b | lead |
| Cracking the Coding Interview | Gayle Laakmann McDowell | 2008 | OL16816263W | The set textbook of Trinity University's interview course and others at Konstanz and Berkeley; it set the candidate side of the whiteboard interview. | https://sites.google.com/trinity.edu/csci3194-f23 | lead |
| Smart and Gets Things Done | Joel Spolsky | 2007 | OL8912201W | It is the classic developer-written guide to recruiting and interviewing programmers. | https://www.librarything.com/work/2807626 | lead |
| Peopleware | Tom DeMarco, Timothy Lister | 1987 | OL38240W | Assigned reading in BYU software engineering courses; the root text on building and keeping software teams. | https://byui-cse.github.io/cse272-course/Prepare/272.01.Prepare.html | lead |
| The Manager's Path | Camille Fournier | 2017 | OL19860807W | On the reading lists of Gergely Orosz and Jacob Kaplan-Moss; it describes the engineering ladder that hiring levels are set against. | https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/my-reading-list/ | lead |
| What Color Is Your Parachute? | Richard N. Bolles | 1970 | OL3333160W | On Time's All-TIME 100 nonfiction list and the Library of Congress list of books that shaped readers' lives; the long-running job-hunting manual. | https://thegreatestbooks.org/v/table/lists/507 | lead |
| The Holloway Guide to Technical Recruiting and Hiring | Osman (Ozzie) Osman et al. | 2020 | OL28057287W | It is the most complete expert-reviewed guide for the employer side. Its first edition is just before 2021. | https://holloway.com/b/technical-recruiting-hiring | lead |
| Talking From 9 to 5 | Deborah Tannen | 1994 | OL2725605W | Taught in The Evergreen State College's Art of Conversation programme and still in print (7 editions, Open Road ebook); the standard account of how conversational style shapes who gets credit, hired and promoted. | https://archives.evergreen.edu/webpages/curricular/2008-2009/artofconversation/node/10/index.html https://openroadmedia.com/ebook/talking-from-9-to-5/9780062210104 | distilled |
| Invisible Women | Caroline Criado Perez | 2019 | OL20157508W | Won both the 2019 Royal Society Science Book Prize and the FT and McKinsey Business Book of the Year; its workplace chapters are the standard popular account of how hiring algorithms, merit ratings and default-male data skew who gets hired. | https://royalsociety.org/news/2019/09/winner-2019-insight-investment-science-writing-prize/ https://www.mckinsey.com/about-us/new-at-mckinsey-blog/2019-business-book-of-the-year-award | distilled |
| Epistemic Injustice: Power and the Ethics of Knowing | Miranda Fricker | 2007 | OL9331207W | The founding account of testimonial and hermeneutical injustice: how prejudice deflates belief in a speaker; the theory under credibility bias in interviews and screening | https://www.uoguelph.ca/arts/sites/default/files/syllabus/W12_PHIL6220_Freedman.pdf https://philo.shs-nancy.univ-lorraine.fr/node/984 https://diversityreadinglist.org/?p=2166 | distilled |
| Conversational Style: Analyzing Talk among Friends | Deborah Tannen | 1984 | - | Tannen's founding study of high-involvement and high-considerateness styles, the basis of Talking From 9 to 5; assigned in sociolinguistics courses (OUP, Biber) and reissued with an afterword in 2005 (reviewed by LINGUIST List). Explains why pace, overlap and pauses get read as competence or its lack. | https://academic.oup.com/book/48215 https://linguistlist.org/issues/17/492/ | distilled |
| You Just Don't Understand | Deborah Tannen | 1990 | - | The book that took the difference view of talk to a mass audience and the main target of its critics; one kind of evidence, kept because its critique literature shows its standing and it supplies the expertise and floor studies. | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Just_Don%27t_Understand | distilled |
| Verbal Hygiene | Deborah Cameron | 1995 | - | Reissued as a Routledge Linguistics Classic and reviewed as a major work of sociolinguistics; its ch. 5 is the standard critique of telling women at work to talk like a man. | https://linguistlist.org/issues/24/2954/ | distilled |
| The Argument Culture | Deborah Tannen | 1998 | - | Names the habit of automatic opposition behind adversarial interviews and reviews; rests on reviews and one reported course use, kept for its vocabulary, and it never discusses hiring. | https://www.biola.edu/winsome-conviction/podcast/2020/today-s-argument-culture | distilled |
| How to Suppress Women's Writing | Joanna Russ | 1983 | isbn:9781477316283 | The named taxonomy of how finished work gets dismissed (credit denied, work reclassified, one achievement standing for all); one kind of evidence, reviews and a 2018 university press reissue with no syllabus found, kept because no other book here names these moves; about literature, so its link to hiring is inference. | https://www.npr.org/2018/04/21/600902444/how-to-suppress-womens-writing-three-decades-old-and-still-sadly-relevant | distilled |
