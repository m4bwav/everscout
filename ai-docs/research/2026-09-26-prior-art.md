# Prior art for everscout (researched 2026-09-26)

## Summary

The closest prior art is the last30days skill (engagement-ranked briefs over 20+ sources). Social listening products that depended on Reddit's paid API died (GummySearch, closing 2026-12-01). Horizon scanning gives the pipeline (observation, signal, cluster, trend) and a scoring rubric. The LLM-wiki pattern gives the storage shape (raw, source notes, entity pages, index, log, lint). Citation studies say URLs must never come from the model. Netnography (Kozinets) is the academic name for scan, immerse, interact, report.

## 1. Agent skills and research agents

- **last30days skill** (github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill, also in trailofbits/skills-curated). Searches 20+ sources (Reddit and HN keyless; X by token or cookies; YouTube via yt-dlp; Polymarket, GitHub, arXiv, Techmeme, Bluesky). Ranks by engagement with relevance floors. Two-phase: discover subreddits and handles, then search them. Merges one story across platforms. Outputs markdown, JSON, HTML, an Atom feed. Optional SQLite `--store` with watchlist and briefing scripts. A public demo showed prompt injection through ingested text, so fetched content is hostile input.
- **STORM / Co-STORM** (github.com/stanford-oval/storm): research from several perspectives, outline, then write with citations; Co-STORM lets a human steer the outline. Fits the baseline study.
- **GPT Researcher**, **LangChain open_deep_research** (supervisor plus parallel researchers). Good breadth and inline citations. Every run is one-shot with no memory.
- **Reddit MCP servers** are mostly read-only (Hawstein/mcp-server-reddit, GeLi2001/reddit-mcp, redditapis-mcp "never posts, comments, votes or DMs"). Read and write kept apart is the pattern.
- **ChatGPT Pulse** (openai.com, 2025-09-25) and **Google Labs CC** (Dec 2025, extended 2026-09-17): the feedback loop (more or less like this, reply to steer) is what improves a daily brief.
- **Feedly Leo**: priorities, deduplication, mute filters. Table stakes.
- Common complaints across the category: invented or wrong sources, shallow listicle summaries, the same story on several days, no memory of what was reported, the loudest thread wins.

## 2. Social listening

- **F5Bot** (f5bot.com): free keyword alerts for Reddit, HN and Lobsters; whole word, exclude terms, required co-terms, subreddit filters. Plain boolean matching is enough.
- **Syften**: alerts only, many platforms, no dashboards.
- **Octolens**: 15+ platforms, relevance and sentiment scoring, API, webhook and MCP on every plan ("own your data").
- **GummySearch** (gummysearch.com/final-chapter): audiences (sets of subreddits), pain points, requests, subreddit discovery. No commercial Reddit licence: sales stopped 2025-11-30, shutdown 2026-12-01.
- For one person what matters: keyword alerts, a mute list, community discovery, request clustering, coarse sentiment, owning the data.

## 3. Horizon scanning and trends

- **UK GO-Science Futures Toolkit** (gov.uk, refreshed June 2023): scan across STEEP or PESTLE for weak signals, then driver mapping and Three Horizons. Criteria: novelty, likelihood, time horizon, uncertainty, impact, relevance.
- **IFTF**: a signal is one concrete, specific example of how the world could differ; a driver is a large established force. Pipeline: item, signal, cluster of three or more independent signals, trend, implication.
- **Nesta** (automated horizon scanning, Innovation Sweet Spots): magnitude times growth across independent source types. Emerging = low magnitude, high growth; hot = both high; stabilising = high magnitude, low growth.
- **Hype cycle critique** (Dedehayir and Steinert 2016; The Economist): Gartner's own data mostly does not follow the curve. Do not assign hype stages; report measured volume and velocity and a judged ring.
- **ThoughtWorks Technology Radar** and Build Your Own Radar: quadrants, rings (Adopt, Trial, Assess, Hold), new or moved markers, a rationale per blip, blips fade if not re-blipped.
- Rubric adopted in `kb/method.md`: novelty, velocity, breadth, evidence, relevance, each 0 to 3; weak signal when novelty 2+, breadth 1 or less, evidence 2+; trend when breadth 3+ and velocity 2+ in two consecutive reports.

## 4. Knowledge-base patterns

- **Karpathy's LLM wiki** (gist 442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f, 2026-04-04): immutable raw sources, an LLM-owned wiki of summaries and entity pages, a schema file; operations ingest, query, lint; `index.md` and an append-only `log.md`.
- Zettelkasten: atomic source notes stay fixed; entity or concept pages accrete.
- Honest counts: canonical ids with an alias table, resolved by code, not by the model; count distinct threads and communities, not mentions; dedupe by canonical URL; exponential time decay with raw counts beside it.
- Claims carry a source note and dates; lint flags stale and contradicting claims.

## 5. Summary faithfulness and citations

- **Tow Center / CJR** (2025-03-06): over 60 percent of answers from eight AI search engines were wrong; over half of Gemini and Grok 3 citations were invented or broken URLs; syndicated copies cited instead of originals.
- **EBU/BBC News Integrity in AI Assistants** (Oct 2025): 45 percent of 3,000+ answers had a significant issue; sourcing the largest category (31 percent).
- **Peters and Chin-Yee** (Royal Society Open Science, Apr 2025): LLM science summaries overgeneralised in up to 73 percent of cases; asking for accuracy made it worse.
- **FABLES** (COLM 2024, arXiv 2404.01261): unfaithful claims are subtle and LLM raters miss them.
- Consequences: URLs only from fetched data; quote the key sentence before paraphrasing; keep hedges and scope ("on my 4090"); structural checks, not "be accurate" prompts.

## 6. Netnography

Kozinets, *Netnography: The Essential Guide to Qualitative Social Media Research* (3rd ed., 2020). Stages: initiation (research questions), collection, integration, communication. Collection has three movements: investigation (search, scout, select, save public traces), immersion (reconnoitre, record, reflect, with an immersion journal) and interaction (interview, involvement, informed consent). AoIR IRE 3.0 (aoir.org/reports/ethics3.pdf, 2019): public is not fair game; verbatim quotes can be searched back to people; identify yourself when interacting.

Mapping: scan = investigation; notes plus the journal = immersion; engagement = interaction; reports and the study = integration and communication.

## 7. Name check (2026-09-26)

`everscout` is free on PyPI, npm and GitHub (a 2023 FRC robotics app once used "EverScout"; its repo is renamed). Bare "scout" is crowded (coleifer/scout, Scout APM, agno-agi/scout), so the CLI, plugin and any MCP server all say `everscout`.
